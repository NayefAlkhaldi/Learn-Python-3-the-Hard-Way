# When it says "the hard way" read "the right way"

> This book is for you if you're looking for something that will quickly and simply explain topics to you. It's one of the finest books I've ever read since the information is presented sequentially, and the exercises and the way the book is laid out encourage the reader to think creatively, understand programming, and find it simple to pick up other programming languages. Despite all these advantages, the book alone is insufficient because the majority of the exercises include brand-new ideas. I believe Python Crash Course to be better because it is more comprehensive and has more tasks to complete than this book. The doesn't means it's bad, **absolutely great**. Well done, Shaw!

## Rating?

> I believe that **8.5/10 is a fair one.**
